## 📐 LinkedIn Image Specifications

| Format | Dimensions | Use Case |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Single image** | 1200 × 627 px | Standard posts |
| **Multi-image** | 1200 × 627 px each | 2-4 image posts |
| **Carousel slide** | 1080 × 1080 px | PDF document upload |
| **Profile banner** | 1584 × 396 px | Profile background |

### Tips for Resizing Screenshots
- Use Canva, Figma, or any image editor
- Add a thin border (2px, light gray) for professional look
- Optional: Add your logo watermark in the corner
- For carousels: Crop to square (1080×1080) and add captions as overlays
