## 📏 LinkedIn Post Best Practices

### Length
- **Optimal:** 1,200–1,500 characters (shows "See more" link which increases engagement)
- **Maximum:** 3,000 characters for detailed posts
- **Hook:** First 2 lines must grab attention (they show before "See more")

### Format
- Use emojis strategically (not every line)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Use → for bullet points (cleaner than •)
- Leave blank lines between sections
- End with a **question** (drives comments)
- Add **CTA** (call to action) with link

### Image Guidelines
- **Single image:** 1200 x 627 px (1.91:1 ratio)
- **Carousel:** 1080 x 1080 px per slide (1:1 ratio)
- **Multi-image:** 2-4 images get more engagement than 1
- **File format:** PNG for screenshots, JPG for photos
- **Carousel PDF:** Convert slides to PDF for document upload

### Engagement Tactics
1. **Reply to every comment** within 1 hour
2. **Ask a question** at the end of every post
3. **Tag relevant people** (max 3-5 per post)
4. **Post consistently** (3-4 times per week)
5. **Engage on others' posts** 30 min before and after your post
6. **Use first comment** for additional links or context
