🚀 **Excited to announce: QuoteFlow AI is LIVE!** 🚀

After months of building, I'm thrilled to launch **QuoteFlow AI** — a full-stack, AI-powered business management platform built specifically for Indian SMBs.

**The problem we're solving:**
Indian small businesses juggle 5+ disconnected tools daily — Excel for tracking, separate billing software, WhatsApp for client communication, manual calculators for estimates. This leads to:

📉 30–50% revenue lost due to underquoting
⏰ Hours wasted on manual data entry
💸 Expensive, non-GST-compliant software
🔄 Constant context switching between apps

**The solution:**
One login. One dashboard. Zero context switching.

Here's what QuoteFlow AI brings to the table:

✨ **AI Estimation Engine** — Describe your project in plain English. AI generates accurate cost breakdowns instantly.

🧾 **GST Invoicing & Billing** — Auto-calculated CGST/SGST/IGST. Tax invoices, proforma invoices, receipts — all in one place.

📊 **Sierra CRM** — Full CRM with Kanban deal pipeline, lead scoring, auto follow-ups, and call logging.

📱 **Marketing Campaigns** — Built-in WhatsApp, Email, SMS blasts with open rate tracking and festival automation.

📈 **Finance & Analytics** — Real-time P&L, cash flow, profit margins, and expense tracking.

🤖 **Dual AI (OpenAI + Gemini)** — Powers quotations, profit optimization, and AI proposals.

🔐 **Enterprise Security** — JWT auth, OAuth2, 2FA, rate limiting, encrypted data at rest.

📲 **Cross-Platform** — Web + Android + iOS via Flutter.

Built with: Java 21 • Spring Boot • React • TypeScript • PostgreSQL • Redis

👉 Check it out: https://quoteflow.ai
