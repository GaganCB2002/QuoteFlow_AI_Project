Project Name: QuoteFlow AI
Link: https://quoteflow.ai

What is the Project?
QuoteFlow AI is a full-stack, AI-powered business management platform for Indian SMBs that unifies quotations, billing, CRM, marketing, and estimation into one dashboard.

Main Features:
- AI Quotation Generator (natural language to itemized quotes)
- GST Invoicing & Billing (auto CGST/SGST/IGST)
- Customer Management (CRM) with Kanban deal pipeline
- Marketing Campaigns (WhatsApp, email, SMS)
- Finance & Analytics (P&L, cash flow, profit margins)
- Document Management & Visitor Tracking
- Mobile apps (Android/iOS via Capacitor)

Motivation:
Indian SMBs were juggling 5+ separate tools (Excel, billing software, CRM, WhatsApp, estimators) leading to manual data entry, expensive non-GST software, and 30-50% underquoting.

Problem Statement:
SMBs lose time and money switching between disconnected tools. QuoteFlow solves this with one login, one dashboard, and zero context switching — powered by AI.
