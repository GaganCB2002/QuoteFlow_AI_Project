# QuoteFlow AI - Project Details & LinkedIn Post

## Detailed Information
**QuoteFlow AI** is a comprehensive, full-stack business management platform designed specifically for Indian Small and Medium Businesses (SMBs). 

**The Problem:** Many Indian SMBs lose valuable time and revenue by constantly switching between disconnected tools. They use Excel for data tracking, separate billing software for invoices, WhatsApp for CRM, and manual calculators for estimations. This leads to heavy manual data entry, the use of expensive non-GST software, and unfortunately, 30-50% underquoting of their services.

**The Solution:** QuoteFlow AI solves this fragmentation by offering a single, unified dashboard with zero context switching, all supercharged by Artificial Intelligence. 

### Main Features
- **AI Quotation Generator:** Converts natural language inputs into professionally itemized quotes instantly.
- **GST Invoicing & Billing:** Fully automated calculation of CGST, SGST, and IGST for compliant billing.
- **Customer Management (CRM):** A visual Kanban deal pipeline to track and manage leads effectively.
- **Marketing Campaigns:** Integrated support for outreach via WhatsApp, email, and SMS.
- **Finance & Analytics:** Comprehensive tracking of P&L, cash flow, and profit margins.
- **Document Management & Visitor Tracking:** Secure storage and visitor logs.
- **Mobile Applications:** Seamless Android and iOS apps built via Capacitor.
