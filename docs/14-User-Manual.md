# User Manual

## QuoteFlow AI – Smart Quotation, Billing, CRM & Marketing Platform

**Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Platforms:** Android (Flutter), Web

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Registration

1. Download QuoteFlow AI from Google Play Store or visit https://app.quoteflow.ai
2. Tap **"Register"**
3. Choose method:
   - **Mobile**: Enter 10-digit number → OTP → Verify
   - **Google**: Tap "Continue with Google" → Select account
4. Enter your name, email, company name
5. Tap **"Create Account"**

### 1.2 Login

1. Open the app or website
2. Enter registered mobile number **or** tap "Continue with Google"
3. If using OTP: Enter OTP received via SMS
4. If using Google: Select your Google account

### 1.3 Quick Tips

- First time? Complete your **Company Profile** first
- You can work **offline** — data syncs when you're back online
- AI helps you create quotations in seconds

## 2. Company Setup

### 2.1 Complete Company Profile

1. After first login, you'll see Company Setup screen
2. Enter:
   - Business Name (required)
   - GST Number (for GST invoices)
   - PAN Number
   - Address (Street, City, State, Pincode)
   - Phone, Email
   - Bank Details (Account Name, Account No, IFSC, Bank Name)
3. Tap **"Save"**

### 2.2 Upload Logo & Signature

1. Go to **Settings → Company Profile**
2. Tap logo placeholder → Select image (JPEG/PNG, max 2MB)
3. Tap signature placeholder → Select signature image
4. Tap **"Save"**

### 2.3 Configure Document Settings

1. Go to **Settings → Document Settings**
2. Set prefixes for:
   - Quotations (default: Q-)
   - Invoices (default: INV-) or GST-, PRO-
   - Receipts (default: RCP-)
3. Set default valid-until days
4. Tap **"Save"**

---

## 3. Managing Customers

### 3.1 Add Customer

1. Tap **"Customers"** from bottom menu
2. Tap **"+"**
3. Enter:
   - Customer Name (required)
   - Phone Number (required)
   - Company Name
   - Email
   - GST Number (system prevents duplicates)
   - Address, City, State, Pincode
4. Tap **"Save"**

### 3.2 View Credit Score

1. Tap on a customer
2. View **Credit Score** badge (0-100)
   - Green (70+): Reliable payer
   - Yellow (40-69): Average
   - Red (<40): Risky — consider advance payment

### 3.3 Duplicate GST Detection

If you try to save a customer with a GSTIN that already exists in your company, the system will show a warning: *"A customer with this GST number already exists."*

## 4. Creating AI-Powered Quotations

### 4.1 AI Quotation Generator (Quick Mode)

1. Tap **"Quotations"** → **"+"** → **"AI Generate"**
2. Enter a brief description, e.g.:
   - *"Website Development for school"*
   - *"SEO package for 6 months"*
   - *"Digital marketing campaign"*
3. Tap **"Generate with AI"**
4. AI generates:
   - Scope of work
   - Itemized pricing
   - Timeline
   - Deliverables
5. Review and edit if needed
6. Select customer (or create new)
7. Tap **"Save"**

### 4.2 AI Profit Optimizer

1. While adding items, tap the **"AI Optimize"** icon
2. Enter your **Cost Price** for the item
3. AI suggests:
   - Recommended Selling Price
   - Your Profit
   - Profit Margin %
   - Maximum Discount You Can Offer
4. Accept suggestion or adjust manually

### 4.3 Voice Quotation

1. Tap the **microphone** icon on quotation screen
2. Speak naturally, e.g.:
   - *"Website for school thirty thousand rupees"*
   - *"Five pages website with hosting forty five thousand"*
3. Tap **"Done"**
4. Review AI-generated quotation from voice
5. Edit if needed and save

### 4.4 AI Proposal Writer

1. Tap **"Quotations"** → **"AI Proposal"**
2. Select proposal type:
   - Software Development Proposal
   - Digital Marketing Proposal
   - AMC Proposal
   - Custom
3. Enter client name and project details
4. Tap **"Generate Proposal"**
5. AI creates a complete professional proposal
6. Review, edit, save, and share

### 4.5 Manual Quotation

1. Tap **"Quotations"** → **"+"** → **"Manual"**
2. Select customer
3. Add items with name, quantity, rate, tax
4. Apply discount if needed
5. Add terms and conditions
6. Tap **"Save"**

## 5. PDF & Sharing

### 5.1 Generate PDF

1. Open any quotation, invoice, or receipt
2. Tap **"Download PDF"**
3. PDF is generated with:
   - Company logo and branding
   - Digital signature
   - Customer details
   - Itemized list
   - Tax breakdown
   - Total
   - Bank details for payment
   - UPI QR code (if applicable)

### 5.2 Share via WhatsApp (One Click)

1. Open document
2. Tap **"Share"** → **WhatsApp**
3. WhatsApp opens with PDF ready to send
4. Select contact and send

### 5.3 Share via Other Methods

- **Email**: Opens email client with PDF
- **Link**: Generates a shareable link
- **Download**: Save PDF to device

---

## 6. Invoices

### 6.1 Convert Quotation to Invoice (One Click)

1. Open an **Accepted** quotation
2. Tap **"Convert to Invoice"**
3. All items, taxes, discounts carried over automatically
4. Select invoice type:
   - **GST Invoice**: For GST-registered businesses
   - **Tax Invoice**: For non-GST billing
   - **Proforma Invoice**: For advance estimates
5. Set due date
6. Tap **"Create Invoice"**

### 6.2 QR Payment

1. Open an invoice
2. Tap **"Generate QR"**
3. UPI QR code is generated — customer can scan and pay
4. Tap **"Share Payment Link"** to send payment link via WhatsApp

### 6.3 E-Signature

1. Open an invoice
2. Tap **"Request Signature"**
3. Share signature link with customer
4. Customer signs on their device
5. Signed PDF is saved automatically

## 7. Receipts

### 7.1 Generate Payment Receipt

1. Open a paid or partially paid invoice
2. Tap **"Generate Receipt"**
3. Select payment type: **Payment Receipt** or **Advance Receipt**
4. Enter payment details
5. Tap **"Generate"**

### 7.2 Advance Receipt

1. Tap **"Receipts"** → **"+"** → **"Advance Receipt"**
2. Select customer
3. Enter amount and payment method
4. Generate and share

## 8. CRM & Follow-Ups

### 8.1 Lead Management

1. Tap **"CRM"** → **"Add Lead"**
2. Select customer or add new
3. Select source: Website, Referral, Social Media, Walk-in, Phone
4. Add notes
5. Update status: New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted/Lost

### 8.2 Smart Follow-Up Engine

The system automatically:
- Reminds you when a quotation is not viewed after 2 days
- Reminds you when an invoice is nearing due date
- Sends follow-up reminders to customers who haven't responded
- Alerts you about overdue invoices

### 8.3 Customer Credit Score

- Calculated automatically based on payment history
- Visible on customer profile
- Helps you decide credit limits

## 9. Marketing Campaigns

### 9.1 WhatsApp Campaign

1. Tap **"Marketing"** → **"New Campaign"** → **WhatsApp**
2. Enter campaign name
3. Write your message
4. Select recipients (from your customers)
5. Choose: **Send Now** or **Schedule for Later**
6. Tap **"Send"**

### 9.2 Festival Wishes

1. Tap **"Marketing"** → **"Festival Wishes"**
2. Select festival (Diwali, Holi, New Year, etc.)
3. Preview auto-generated greeting
4. Select recipients
5. Schedule or send now

### 9.3 Campaign Analytics

After sending, view:
- Total sent
- Successfully delivered
- Opened (for emails)
- Response rate

## 11. Offline Mode

- QuoteFlow AI works **without internet**
- All data is saved locally
- When internet is restored, data **syncs automatically**
- Green checkmark = Synced | Yellow clock = Pending | Red X = Error

---

## 12. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| OTP not received | Check network, tap "Resend OTP" after 60 seconds |
| WhatsApp not available | App shows fallback message: Share via Email or Download |
| PDF not generating | Check internet (or switch to offline mode), retry |
| Sync pending | Ensure internet is active, pull down to refresh |
| AI not responding | Check OpenAI/Gemini API status, fallback to manual mode |
| Duplicate GST warning | Customer with same GSTIN already exists in your company |
