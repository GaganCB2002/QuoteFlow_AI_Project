Platform Features

Everything you need to run your business

From AI-powered quotations to marketing automation — one platform replaces Excel, billing software, CRM, and WhatsApp.

AI Quotation Generator

Describe your service once. AI generates a complete quotation with scope, pricing, timeline, and deliverables in under 30 seconds.

AI Profit Optimizer

Enter your cost. AI suggests optimal selling price, profit margin, and maximum discount limit based on market data and historical trends.

GST Billing

Generate GST invoices, tax invoices, and proforma invoices with auto-calculated CGST, SGST, IGST. Fully compliant with Indian tax regulations.

WhatsApp Sharing

One-click share quotations, invoices, and receipts via WhatsApp. No manual downloading or email attachments needed. Customers receive instantly.

CRM & Follow-Ups

Track leads, log customer interactions, and let the auto-follow-up engine remind you and your customers when action is needed.

Marketing Campaigns

WhatsApp bulk campaigns, email newsletters, SMS blasts, and automated festival wishes. Target the right customers with the right message.

Voice Quotation

Speak naturally — "Website for school ₹30,000" — and the system creates a complete quotation from your voice. No typing required.

E-Signature

Customers can sign quotations and invoices digitally. No printing, scanning, or physical paperwork required. Legally compliant.

Business Analytics

Revenue reports, profit analysis, conversion rates, top-performing services, and customer lifetime value. Data-driven business decisions.

Offline Mode

Work without internet access. Data syncs automatically when you're back online. No more lost quotations due to connectivity issues.

Customer Credit Score

Auto-calculated credit score based on payment history. Green/Yellow/Red indicators help you decide credit limits for each customer.

QR Payment

Generate UPI QR codes and payment links for quotations and invoices. Customers can scan and pay instantly from any UPI app.
